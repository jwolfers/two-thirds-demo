How the game works

Your best guess depends on
what you think everyone else will guess.

If I think the class average will be…
50
↓ multiply by ⅔ ↓
…then I should guess
33.3
Step 1 The average can't be more than 100.
Step 2 So ⅔ of the average can't be more than 66.7.
Step 3 So any guess above 66.7 is dominated: guessing 66.7 always does at least as well.
Economists call a choice dominated when another choice is always at least as good. Picking one is like ordering the gas-station sushi.

Nash equilibrium

Keep thinking, and the numbers collapse to zero.

Nash equilibrium: a situation where nobody can do better by changing their own choice, given what everyone else is doing.
