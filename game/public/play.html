The ⅔ Game · Michigan Edition

Go Blue, Guess Low

How it works

  1. Pick a number from 0 to 100.
  2. We average everyone's numbers.
  3. Whoever is closest to ⅔ of the average wins.

Ties share the glory. Decimals are allowed. Prizes are mostly bragging rights, which is more than Michigan football has this year.

Remind me of the rules

Everyone picks a number from 0 to 100. Take the average of all the picks, then multiply by ⅔. Closest guess wins. You can change your guess as many times as you like until the professor blows the whistle.
