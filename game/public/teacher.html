Average guess

Newspaper contests, 1997–98, 1,476–3,696 readers each. Bosch-Domènech, Montalvo, Nagel & Satorra, AER (2002).

  1. Knowing the equilibrium isn't the same as winning. Zero is only the best guess if everyone else plays it too.
  2. What you need is common knowledge of rationality. I'm rational, you're rational, I know you're rational, you know I know… Most people stop after a step or two.
  3. Keynes's beauty contest. In markets you win by predicting what others will think, not what's "really" best. (General Theory, 1936, ch. 12)
