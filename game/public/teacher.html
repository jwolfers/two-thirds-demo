How did you think?

Level-k thinking: how many steps ahead?

Level 0 picks without thinking. On average: 50.

Level 1 thinks “everyone else is Level 0,” so guesses ⅔ × 50 = 33.

Level 2 thinks everyone else is Level 1: ⅔ × 33 = 22.

Each extra level multiplies by another ⅔.

Winning target?

The best answer, given what everyone else did.

The lesson: your best move depends on what you believe others will do. Keynes compared investing to a beauty contest where you win by picking the face others will pick, not the one you like best.

The evidence: in Rosemarie Nagel's 1995 lab experiments, first-round averages landed around 35. When the Financial Times ran this game in 1997, the winning number was 13.
